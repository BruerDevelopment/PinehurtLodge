Rollinsville, the wonderful small rural town of less than 200 people and home to our Pinehurst Lodge. Nestled in the picturesque Colorado Rockies, Rollinsville offers 
a blend of outdoor adventure, artisanal flavors, and unique local culture. Whether you're a local looking for a new spot 
or a visitor seeking hidden gems, here are five experiences you won't want to miss in Rollinsville.

## 1. Gold Dirt Distillery
At Gold Dirt Distillery, elevation isn't the only thing that's high—so are the spirits and cocktails crafted here. 
This artisanal distillery is known for its premium spirits and breathtaking views. Stop by their tasting room to 
sample their creations, relax, and savor the essence of the Rockies in every sip.

Hours of Opperation: Wed-Thur 4-8ish, Fri-Sat 3-9ish, Sun 3-8ish

## 2. Toss: Wood Fired Eatery
Craving wood-fired pizza and flavorful small plates? Toss is the place to be. Located at 8000 feet above sea level, 
Toss offers a cozy atmosphere where you can enjoy delicious seasonal dishes paired with craft drinks. 
Whether you're dining indoors or on their outdoor patio, expect a culinary experience that's as elevated as the altitude.

Hours of Opperation: Wed/Thur/Fri 3-8, Sat/Sun 12-8

## 3. Howlin Wind Brewing & Blending
A true gem of Rollinsville, Howlin Wind Brewing & Blending is more than just a brewery—it's a community hub where classic, 
innovative, and funky beers come to life. Step into this local favorite, meet the passionate brewers, and sample their 
latest creations. It's the perfect place to unwind after a day of exploring.

Hours of Opperation: Sun 1-8, Mon/Wed/Thur 2-8, Tue 4-9, Fri 2-9, Sat 1-9

## 4. Melt Coffee Microroastery & Espresso Bar
For coffee aficionados, Melt Coffee Microroastery & Espresso Bar offers a haven of rich aromas and expertly brewed coffee. 
Located inside Howlin Wind, this espresso bar specializes in micro-roasted beans, ensuring each cup is a flavorful delight.

Hours of Opperation: Mon/Thur/Fri 8-3, Sat/Sun 8-5

## 5. Moffat Tunnel
Dive into history with a visit to the Moffat Tunnel, a marvel of engineering that cuts through the Continental Divide. 
This historic railroad and water tunnel not only offers a glimpse into Colorado's past but also provides a scenic 
backdrop for hiking, photography, and exploring the natural beauty of the area.

Hours of Opperation: Open 24 / 7. but best in the daylight!

Whether you're indulging in local flavors, exploring the great outdoors, or delving into the town's history, 
Rollinsville promises a memorable experience at every turn. Plan your visit today and discover why this mountain town 
captures the hearts of all who visit.

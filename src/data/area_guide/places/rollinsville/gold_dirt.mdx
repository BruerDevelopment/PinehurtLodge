# Gold Dirt Distillery
Just a short walk from the Pinehurst Lodge awaits Gold Dirt Distillery offering small batch, 
craft spirits with historic, small-town charm. Named after the mining claim (Gold Dirt Mines) 
of Rollinsville’s founder, John Q. A. Rollins, the Gold Dirt Distillery sits right in the heart 
of Gold Dirt mining area. 

Open Wed-Sun.
